# Phase 1: DOD Prime Contractor Research & Outreach

Welcome to Phase 1 of your DOD Prime Contractor partnership initiative! This repository contains all the resources, templates, and guidance you need to successfully identify and connect with top DOD Prime Contractors for mentorship and partnership opportunities.

## 🎯 Phase 1 Objectives

1. **Research** major DOD Prime Contractors from the approved mentor list
2. **Identify** your top 5 target companies based on capability alignment
3. **Prepare** professional outreach materials (emails, phone scripts, capability statements)
4. **Connect** with decision-makers at target companies
5. **Establish** relationships that can lead to mentor-protégé partnerships or subcontracting opportunities

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Review the DOD Prime Contractors List (30 minutes)
📄 **File**: `dod_primes/DOD_PRIME_CONTRACTORS_LIST.md`

- Review the list of 15 major DOD Prime Contractors
- Understand their focus areas and key programs
- Note which companies align with your capabilities

### Step 2: Document Your Qualifications (1-2 hours)
📄 **File**: `qualifications/COMPANY_QUALIFICATIONS_TEMPLATE.md`

- Fill out your company information
- List all certifications (CMMC, ISO, etc.)
- Document your capabilities and past performance
- Prepare your value proposition

### Step 3: Research Your Top 5 Companies (2-4 hours)
📄 **File**: `company_profiles/COMPANY_RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md`

For each of your top 5 selected companies:
- Use the template to create a detailed profile
- Research on USASpending.gov for their contracts
- Identify their mentor-protégé program details
- Map your capabilities to their needs
- Find contact information for key personnel

### Step 4: Prepare Outreach Materials (2-3 hours)
📄 **Files**: 
- `outreach_templates/EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md`
- `outreach_templates/PHONE_SCRIPTS.md`

- Customize email templates for each company
- Prepare phone scripts with company-specific talking points
- Practice your elevator pitch
- Gather all supporting documents (capability statement, certifications)

### Step 5: Execute the Action Plan (4-6 weeks)
📄 **File**: `PHASE1_ACTION_PLAN.md`

- Follow the week-by-week plan
- Track all outreach activities
- Maintain consistent follow-up
- Document all interactions

## 💡 Best Practices

### Research Phase
- ✅ Use USASpending.gov to find recent contract awards
- ✅ Check company websites for mentor-protégé program information
- ✅ Review company news and press releases
- ✅ Look up contacts on LinkedIn
- ✅ Understand their current programs and challenges

### Outreach Phase
- ✅ Personalize every communication
- ✅ Lead with value you can provide
- ✅ Be professional and confident
- ✅ Follow up consistently (but respectfully)
- ✅ Keep detailed notes of all interactions
- ✅ Respond promptly to any inquiries

### Communication Tips
- ✅ Email on Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 1-3 PM
- ✅ Call on Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM
- ✅ Follow up every 7-10 days
- ✅ Maximum 3 follow-ups before pausing
- ✅ Always include specific next steps

## 🎓 CMMC & Compliance

All DOD contractors and subcontractors must meet CMMC (Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification) requirements. Ensure you:

- [ ] Know your current CMMC level
- [ ] Document your compliance status
- [ ] Understand requirements for your target level
- [ ] Can speak confidently about your cybersecurity posture
- [ ] Have certificates and documentation ready to share

**Key Compliance Areas**:
- CMMC Level 1, 2, or 3 certification
- NIST 800-171 compliance
- Security clearances (if applicable)
- Facility clearance (if applicable)
- ISO certifications (9001, 27001, etc.)

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- ❌ Sending generic, template emails
- ❌ Focusing on what you need instead of what you offer
- ❌ Not researching the company before contact
- ❌ Following up too frequently or aggressively
- ❌ Missing deadlines or commitments
- ❌ Failing to track interactions
- ❌ Giving up after one or two attempts
- ❌ Being unprepared for phone calls
- ❌ Not having materials ready to send

## 🎯 Selection Criteria for Top 5

When choosing your top 5 companies, consider:

1. **Capability Alignment**: Do your skills match their needs?
2. **Active Mentor Programs**: Do they have established programs?
3. **Geographic Fit**: Proximity or remote work feasibility?
4. **Contract Opportunities**: Are they winning contracts in your area?
5. **Cultural Fit**: Do values and approach align?
6. **Size Match**: Is company size appropriate for partnership?
7. **Recent Activity**: Are they actively growing or hiring in your domain?

## 📞 Contact Information Priority

When researching contacts, look for:

1. **First Priority**: Mentor-Protégé Program Manager
2. **Second Priority**: Small Business Liaison Officer (SBLO)
3. **Third Priority**: Business Development Director
4. **Fourth Priority**: Relevant Program Manager
5. **Fifth Priority**: General business development contact
