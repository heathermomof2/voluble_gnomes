This is your quick reference for getting started immediately. For detailed information, see the full documentation in each folder.

### Hour 4: Make Contact
1. Send first email to Company #1
2. Find their phone number
3. Review phone script
4. Make the call (or schedule for tomorrow morning)

## 📞 First Contact Checklist

Before reaching out to any company:
- [ ] I know their recent contract awards
- [ ] I can name 2-3 of their key programs
- [ ] I've identified the right contact person
- [ ] I have their email and phone number
- [ ] I can articulate my value proposition in 30 seconds
- [ ] I have my capability statement ready to send
- [ ] I have my calendar open for scheduling
- [ ] I'm prepared and confident

## 🎯 Your 30-Second Elevator Pitch

Practice this before any call:

> "We are a [CMMC Level X] certified small business specializing in [primary capability]. We have [X years] of experience supporting [relevant programs/agencies] and have achieved [specific notable result]. I believe we could provide valuable support to [Company Name]'s work in [specific program area], particularly in [specific capability area]."

Customize the bracketed sections for each company.

## 📧 Email Template - Ready to Use

**Subject**: CMMC-Certified Small Business Seeking Mentorship Partnership

**Body**:
```
Dear [Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am the [Title] at [Your Company], a CMMC Level [X] certified small business specializing in [core capability].

I am reaching out to explore potential partnership opportunities through the DOD Mentor-Protégé Program. After researching [Company Name]'s work on [specific program], I believe our capabilities could provide meaningful support to your team.

Our key qualifications:
• CMMC Level [X] Certified
• [X] years supporting [relevant domain]
• Expertise in [capability 1], [capability 2], and [capability 3]
• Strong past performance with [reference if relevant]

I would welcome a brief call to discuss how we might work together. I'm available [suggest 2-3 times] or happy to work around your schedule.

Thank you for considering this opportunity.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Phone]
[Email]
```

## ☎️ Phone Script - Ready to Use

**Opening**:
> "Good [morning/afternoon], this is [Your Name] calling from [Your Company]. May I speak with [Target Name] or the person responsible for your mentor-protégé program?"

**If connected**:
> "Thank you for taking my call. I'll be brief. We're a CMMC-certified small business specializing in [capability], and I'm reaching out because we could support [Company Name]'s work in [area]. Do you have 3 minutes?"

**If YES**:
> "Great! We specialize in [capability] and have [X years] supporting [domain]. What caught my attention was [recent news/contract]. We excel at [specific thing] and have achieved [metric]. I'd like to explore partnership opportunities. What would be the best next step?"

**Closing**:
> "Perfect. I'll send you our capability statement today. What's the best email? And may I follow up with you in [7-10 days] to answer any questions?"

## 🗓️ Daily Routine

**Morning (15 min)**:
- Check email for responses
- Review today's outreach plan
- Prepare for any scheduled calls

**Mid-Day (30-60 min)**:
- Make phone calls (best time: 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM)
- Send follow-up emails
- Research next contacts

**End of Day (15 min)**:
- Log all activities
- Send promised materials
- Plan tomorrow

## 🚨 Critical Success Factors

1. **Be Specific**: Name their programs, reference their recent wins
2. **Be Brief**: Respect their time - get to the point
3. **Be Valuable**: Lead with what you offer, not what you need
4. **Be Professional**: Perfect grammar, polite tone, proper format
5. **Be Persistent**: Follow up 2-3 times before moving on
6. **Be Prepared**: Have materials ready, know your talking points

## 🔍 Quick Research Sources

**Before contacting any company**:
1. Visit their website → Find small business/mentor program page
2. Check USASpending.gov → Search company name → See recent contracts
3. LinkedIn → Find contacts with titles: "Small Business Liaison", "Mentor Program Manager", "Business Development"
4. Google News → Search "[Company name] contract award" for recent wins

## 💡 Pro Tips

✅ **DO**:
- Call on Tuesday-Thursday mornings
- Send emails on Tuesday-Thursday between 8-10 AM
- Customize every message
- Track everything in a spreadsheet
- Follow up exactly when you say you will

❌ **DON'T**:
- Use generic templates without customization
- Call on Monday mornings or Friday afternoons
- Follow up more than 3 times without response
- Promise what you can't deliver
- Share sensitive information via unsecured email

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**"I can't find the right contact"**
→ Call main number, ask for small business liaison

**"I'm not getting responses"**
→ Try calling instead of just emailing

**"I don't know what to say"**
→ Use the scripts provided, practice before calling

**"I'm nervous about calling"**
→ Start with email, practice script with friend, remember they're just people

**"I don't have all certifications"**
→ Lead with what you DO have, explain what you're pursuing
