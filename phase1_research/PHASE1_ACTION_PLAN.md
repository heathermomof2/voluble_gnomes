## Common Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: Not getting responses
**Solutions**:
- Try different times of day
- Try different contacts at same company
- Improve subject lines (A/B test)
- Add more value in initial message
- Connect on LinkedIn first
- Find warm introduction path

### Challenge: "Send me information" brush-off
**Solutions**:
- Send information promptly and professionally
- Include a specific call to action
- Propose specific next step and timing
- Ask clarifying questions before sending
- Follow up consistently

### Challenge: Company says "not the right time"
**Solutions**:
- Ask when would be better timing
- Request permission to stay in touch
- Offer to provide value in meantime
- Set calendar reminder to follow up
- Connect on LinkedIn to stay visible

### Challenge: Can't find right contact
**Solutions**:
- Check LinkedIn for title keywords
- Call main line and ask who handles small business partnerships
- Check SAM.gov for small business liaisons
- Attend company events or webinars
- Look for recent press releases with names
- Join relevant industry associations
