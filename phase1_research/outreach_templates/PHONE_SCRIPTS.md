# Phone Script Templates for DOD Prime Contractor Outreach

## Script 1: Cold Call - Initial Contact

### Pre-Call Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Research the company and specific contact
- [ ] Review recent company news/contract awards
- [ ] Prepare your 30-second elevator pitch
- [ ] Have capability statement ready to reference
- [ ] Have calendar open for scheduling
- [ ] Pen and paper for notes
- [ ] Confident, professional mindset

### Opening (First 30 seconds)

```
"Good [morning/afternoon], this is [Your Name] calling from [Your Company Name]. 

May I speak with [Target Name] or the person responsible for your 
mentor-protégé program / small business partnerships?"

[If you reach the right person:]

"Thank you for taking my call, [Name]. I'll be brief and respectful of 
your time. We are a [certification status] small business specializing 
in [core capability], and I'm reaching out because we believe we could 
provide valuable support to [Their Company Name]'s work in [specific 
program area].

Do you have 3-5 minutes for me to explain how we might be able to help?"
```

### If YES - Continue with Pitch

```
"Excellent, thank you. Let me quickly share what we do and why I thought 
we might be a good fit:

[Your Company Name] specializes in [primary capability 1], [primary 
capability 2], and [primary capability 3]. We're CMMC Level [X] certified 
and have [X years] of experience supporting [relevant agencies or programs].

What caught my attention about [Their Company Name] was [specific recent 
news, contract, or program]. Based on that, I see potential alignment in 
[specific area].

We excel at [specific capability] and have achieved [specific metric or 
accomplishment]. For example, [brief success story - 2 sentences max].

I'd like to explore whether there might be opportunities to support your 
team either through the mentor-protégé program or as a subcontractor on 
[relevant programs].

Does this sound like something worth exploring further?"
```

### If YES to Further Discussion

```
"Wonderful. What would be the best next step? I can:

A) Send you a detailed capability statement and past performance summary 
   for your review
B) Schedule a more in-depth call to discuss specific opportunities
C) Both - send materials and schedule a follow-up call

Which would you prefer?"

[Get commitment and details:]

"Perfect. And what's the best email address to send that to?"
[Record email]

[If scheduling call:]
"Great. I have availability on [Date/Time Option 1], [Date/Time Option 2], 
or [Date/Time Option 3]. Which works best for you?"
[Schedule and confirm]

"Excellent. I'll send a calendar invite and the materials we discussed. 
Thank you so much for your time, [Name]. I look forward to speaking again 
on [date]."
```

### If NO - But Interested in Materials

```
"I completely understand. Would it be helpful if I sent you a brief 
capability statement and information about our qualifications? Then you 
can review at your convenience and reach out if you see potential alignment.

What's the best email address for that?"

[After getting email:]

"Perfect. I'll send that over today along with my direct contact information. 
If you have any questions or would like to discuss further, please don't 
hesitate to reach out. 

May I follow up with you in [7-10 days] to see if you've had a chance to 
review?

Thank you for your time, [Name]. Have a great day."
```

### If NO - Not Interested

```
"I appreciate your honesty. Before I let you go, is there someone else at 
[Company Name] who might be the right person to discuss small business 
partnerships or subcontracting opportunities?

[If they provide a name:]
"Thank you so much. May I mention that you suggested I reach out to them?
What's the best way to contact [new contact name]?"

[If no referral:]
"I understand. Thank you for your time. May I reach back out in [3-6 months] 
in case priorities change?

Thank you again, and I wish you continued success."
```

## Script 2: Gatekeeper Handling

### When Reaching an Assistant/Receptionist

```
"Good [morning/afternoon], this is [Your Name] with [Your Company Name] 
calling for [Target Name].

I'm following up on a matter regarding potential partnership opportunities 
for DOD programs. Is [he/she/they] available?"
```

### If Asked "What is this regarding?"

```
"We're a CMMC-certified small business that specializes in [capability], 
and we're exploring potential mentor-protégé or subcontracting partnerships. 
I wanted to discuss how we might support [Their Company Name]'s work in 
[specific area].

Is [Target Name] the right person to speak with about this, or would you 
recommend someone else?"
```

### If Asked to Send Email Instead

```
"Absolutely, I'd be happy to do that. What's the best email address?

[Record email]

And just to make sure it gets proper attention, is there any specific 
information I should include in the subject line or anything else I should 
mention?"

[After getting info:]

"Perfect. I'll send that over today. May I ask - what's the typical 
response time, and would it be appropriate for me to follow up with a 
call if I don't hear back within [X days]?

Thank you so much for your help. May I get your name?"

[Record name]

"Thank you, [Name]. I appreciate your assistance."
```

## Script 3: Follow-Up Call (After Sending Email)

### Opening

```
"Good [morning/afternoon], this is [Your Name] calling from [Your Company Name]. 

I'm following up on an email I sent to [Name] on [date] regarding 
partnership opportunities. Is [he/she/they] available?

[If yes, when connected:]

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Company Name]. I wanted to 
follow up on the email I sent on [date] about potential partnership 
opportunities. Did you have a chance to review the information I sent?"
```

### If YES - They Reviewed

```
"Excellent. What questions can I answer for you?

[Address their questions]

What do you see as the next steps from here?"

[Listen and respond accordingly]
```

### If NO - Haven't Reviewed Yet

```
"No problem, I know you're busy. Let me give you a 30-second recap of 
why I reached out:

[Brief value proposition - 30 seconds]

Would it be helpful if I resent that email, or would you prefer I follow 
up at a better time?

[If follow-up:]
"What timing would work better for you - a week from now, two weeks?"

[Confirm and schedule]
```

## Script 5: Scheduled Call / In-Depth Discussion

### Opening

```
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Company Name]. Thanks for 
making time to speak with me today.

[If they scheduled it:]
I know we have [X minutes] scheduled. Is that still good for you?

[Confirm they have time]

Great. I thought we could start with a brief overview of our capabilities, 
then I'd love to learn more about [Their Company Name]'s current priorities 
and challenges, and then we can explore where there might be mutual benefit. 
Does that work for you?"
```

### Discovery Questions

Use these to understand their needs:

```
1. "Can you tell me about your current priorities in [specific area]?"

2. "What are the biggest challenges you're facing with [specific program/area]?"

3. "Are there any capability gaps you're looking to fill through partnerships 
   or subcontracting?"

4. "What does your ideal mentor-protégé relationship look like?"

5. "Are there specific contract vehicles or programs where you actively seek 
   small business partners?"

6. "What qualities do you look for in a subcontractor or partner?"

7. "What's your timeline for making decisions about new partnerships?"

8. "Beyond technical capabilities, what else is important to you in a 
   business partner?"
```

### Closing the Call

```
"This has been a very productive conversation. Let me summarize what I 
heard:

[Summarize 2-3 key points]

And the next steps we agreed on are:
1. [Action item 1]
2. [Action item 2]
3. [Action item 3]

Do I have that right? Is there anything else we should add?

[Confirm next steps]

I'll [specific action] by [specific date], and we'll reconnect on [date/time].

Thank you so much for your time, [Name]. I'm excited about the potential 
to work together.

[If appropriate:] Have a great rest of your [day/week]!"
```

## Handling Common Objections

### "We're not taking on new partners right now"

```
"I understand. Can you share what would need to change for the timing to 
be right? And would it be okay if I stay in touch and reach back out in 
[timeframe] to check if the situation has changed?

Also, even if a formal partnership isn't possible now, would it make sense 
for us to stay connected for potential future opportunities?"
```

### "We handle everything internally"

```
"That's great that you have such strong internal capabilities. I'm curious - 
are there ever situations where external support could help during peak 
periods, or for specialized capabilities that might not make sense to 
maintain full-time?

[If yes:] "Tell me more about that..."

[If no:] "I understand. Would it be okay to stay in touch in case that 
changes?"
```

### "We already have subcontractors for that"

```
"That's good to hear. Are you happy with your current partners in that area?

[If yes:] "That's excellent. Are there other areas where you might need 
support?

[If they express any dissatisfaction:] "What would you need from a partner 
to improve on that situation?"
```

### "Send me information and we'll review"

```
"Absolutely, I'll do that. To make sure I send the most relevant information, 
can I ask - what specifically should I focus on? [Pause for answer]

And what's your typical review process and timeline?

May I follow up with you in [timeframe based on their answer] to answer 
any questions?"
```

### "We only work with companies that have X clearance/certification/size"

```
[If you have it:] "We actually do have [that qualification]. [Provide brief detail]."

[If you don't:] "I appreciate you sharing that requirement. Is that an 
absolute requirement for all partnerships, or are there situations where 
you might work with a company that's in the process of obtaining [qualification]?

We're actively pursuing [qualification] and expect to have it by [timeframe]. 
Would it make sense to reconnect once we have that in place?"
```

## Call Preparation Checklist

Before every call, prepare:

- [ ] **Company Research**
  - Recent news and press releases
  - Recent contract awards (USASpending.gov)
  - Active programs and initiatives
  - Company challenges or gaps

- [ ] **Contact Research**
  - LinkedIn profile review
  - Role and responsibilities
  - How long with company
  - Previous experience

- [ ] **Your Materials Ready**
  - Capability statement
  - Past performance examples
  - Certifications list
  - Specific metrics and achievements
  - Calendar for scheduling

- [ ] **Environment**
  - Quiet location
  - Good phone connection
  - Note-taking materials
  - Company website open for reference
  - Your confidence and energy high

---

## Post-Call Checklist

After every call:

- [ ] **Immediate Notes**
  - Key points discussed
  - Commitments made (yours and theirs)
  - Next steps and deadlines
  - Personal details mentioned (kids, hobbies, etc.)

- [ ] **Follow-Up Email**
  - Send within 4 hours of call
  - Summarize discussion
  - Confirm next steps
  - Attach promised materials

- [ ] **CRM/Tracking Update**
  - Log call details
  - Set reminders for follow-up actions
  - Update contact status

- [ ] **Material Preparation**
  - Prepare any promised materials
  - Customize based on discussion
  - Have ready before next interaction

## Key Success Factors

### Tone and Delivery
- **Confident but humble** - You bring value, but you're not arrogant
- **Energetic but professional** - Enthusiasm is contagious
- **Clear and concise** - Respect their time
- **Listening > Talking** - Ask questions, hear their needs

### Pacing
- Speak at moderate pace
- Pause after asking questions
- Don't rush through your pitch
- Let silence work for you

### Energy Level
- Stand while calling (increases energy)
- Smile (they can hear it)
- Vary your tone
- Match their energy level

### Professionalism
- Use their name 2-3 times during conversation
- Take notes
- Never interrupt
- Thank them sincerely

### Follow-Through
- Do what you say you'll do
- Meet deadlines
- Be responsive
- Be persistent but respectful
