## Email Best Practices

### DO:
- ✓ Research the recipient and company thoroughly
- ✓ Personalize each email - reference specific programs, contracts, or news
- ✓ Be concise and professional
- ✓ Focus on value you can provide to them
- ✓ Include specific, quantifiable achievements
- ✓ Proofread carefully
- ✓ Follow up consistently but respectfully (7-10 day intervals)
- ✓ Use professional email signature with all contact information
- ✓ Attach relevant documents (capability statement, certifications)

### DON'T:
- ✗ Use generic, mass-email templates
- ✗ Make it all about what you need
- ✗ Oversell or exaggerate capabilities
- ✗ Send too many follow-ups (max 3)
- ✗ Use informal language or emojis
- ✗ Send without proofreading
- ✗ Include large attachments (keep under 5MB total)
- ✗ CC multiple people at the same company on first contact

### Timing:
- **Best Days**: Tuesday - Thursday
- **Best Times**: 8-10 AM or 1-3 PM (recipient's time zone)
- **Avoid**: Mondays (inbox overload), Fridays (weekend focus), holidays
- **Follow-up Cadence**: 
  - First follow-up: 7-10 days
  - Second follow-up: 14 days after first
  - Third follow-up: 21 days after second (final attempt)

### Subject Line Tips:
- Keep it under 50 characters if possible
- Be specific and professional
- Include your company name
- Avoid spam trigger words (free, guarantee, act now, etc.)
- Make it clear what you're offering/requesting

### Attachment Guidelines:
- **Always include**: Capability Statement (1-2 pages)
- **Often helpful**: Company Profile, Certifications Summary
- **For follow-ups**: Past Performance Examples, Technical White Papers
- **Format**: PDF (most compatible and professional)
- **Naming**: "CompanyName_DocumentType_Date.pdf"
