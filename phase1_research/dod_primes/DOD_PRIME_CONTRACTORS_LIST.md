## Selection Criteria for Top 5

When selecting your top 5 companies for outreach, consider:

1. **Alignment with Your Capabilities**
   - Match your skills and certifications with their needs
   - Identify specific divisions or programs where you can add value

2. **Active Mentor Programs**
   - Companies with established mentor-protégé programs
   - Track record of successful partnerships

3. **Geographic Considerations**
   - Proximity to their facilities or willingness to work remotely
   - Regional office locations

4. **Contract Opportunities**
   - Current and upcoming contract awards
   - Areas where they need subcontractor support

5. **Cultural Fit**
   - Company values alignment
   - Size and structure compatibility
   - Innovation focus
